04 · Science notes

What the ingredients do

Plant fibres help provide structure. Tea and coffee contain organic compounds worth investigating. Starch helps bind the mixture.

What the test shows

A comparison of visible colour in the effluent from two test setups, provided the experiment is controlled and recorded consistently.

What needs further testing

Heavy-metal immobilisation requires suitable metal-specific chemical analysis, repeated trials and teacher-approved safety procedures.

Prototype method

  1. Prepare dried and chopped date-palm fibres.
  2. Mix the processed fibres with spent tea, coffee grounds and cooked starch paste.
  3. Press the mixture into a mat and allow it to dry fully.
  4. Compare the control and mat setups using the same soil mass, water volume and timing.
